The Zorathian Sun is a yellow star at the center of the Veloria system. It is about 4.2 billion years old and burns steadily, with a surface temperature of roughly 5,900 degrees Celsius.

Every 11 years the Zorathian Sun goes through a solar storm cycle called the Great Flare. During a Great Flare, auroras can be seen near the equator of the inner planets.

The Sun's light takes exactly 9 minutes to reach the planet Kelmora, which is the innermost planet.
