The Veloria Survey Probe was launched in the year 2031 and was the first spacecraft to visit Tessaly. It carried a camera, a magnetometer, and a dust detector.

The probe reached Tessaly after a journey of 6 years. Its main discovery was that Tessaly has a faint ring system made of dark dust.

Mission control for the Veloria Survey Probe was located in the city of Port Almera, and the mission ended in 2041 when the probe's fuel ran out.
