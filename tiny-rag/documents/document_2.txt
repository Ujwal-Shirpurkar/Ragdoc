Kelmora is a small, rocky planet with no moons. Its surface is covered in black basalt, and daytime temperatures reach 410 degrees Celsius.

Tessaly is the largest planet in the Veloria system. It is a gas giant with 14 moons and a pale green atmosphere made mostly of hydrogen and methane.
